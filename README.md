### Featured Projects

**🔧 [Odoo Garage Blockchain](https://github.com/Soukaina009/odoo-custom-auto-repair)**
Custom Odoo module for auto repair shops. It manages mechanics, vehicles, and repairs, and anchors every completed repair on the Ethereum blockchain (Sepolia testnet) for tamper-proof traceability.

**📊 [ML Methods Hub](https://github.com/Soukaina009/ML_Methods_Hub)**
Seven classification and dimensionality reduction methods (LDA, QDA, PCA, KNN, Decision Trees, SVM) compared on the MNIST dataset. Best result: 96.36% accuracy with a PCA + SVM pipeline.

**📡 [Compressed Sensing for MNIST](https://github.com/Soukaina009/compressed-sensing-mnist-classification)**
A comparison between classic LDA classification and Compressed Sensing on MNIST. The compressed approach cuts data volume by 68% while losing only 1.5 points of accuracy. Includes a full written report on the theory and results.
